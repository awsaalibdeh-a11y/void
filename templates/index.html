Welcome to VOID City

  1. Drive: W A S D or the arrows. Space handbrake to drift, Shift nitro. Thousands of cars, cabs, buses and people.
  2. Smash: hit traffic faster than it is going and it breaks apart. Anything faster or heavier than you does the damage. Hit a wall hard and you get hurt too.
  3. Fly: F. Slow, the thrusters hover (Space up, Q down). Fast, the wings fly for real: A/D bank to turn, Space/Q pitch, too slow and it stalls.
  4. Stunts & missions: the loop and ramps, gold rings in the sky, near misses, drifts, and green pads on the park lawn and skyscraper rooftops.
  1. Police, races, online: smash cars and the police come for you (lose them or get busted). Drive into a 🏁 marker and press Enter to race. Online plays with real people, with voice chat.
  2. GPS & autopilot: M or tap the map to pick a place, O autopilot drives (or flies) you there. L lands on a glide slope.
