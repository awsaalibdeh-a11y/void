Fly the Hydra

  1. Drive: hold W / ↑ (Gas). Too slow at the top of the loop and you fall.
  2. Ramp: hit it flat out and you take off.
  3. Fly: Space thrust lifts you, W boosts, A D steer. The wings open on their own at speed.
  4. Land: follow the arrow to a green pad. S brakes, so touch down under 6 m/s.

C switches to the front seat. On a phone, use the buttons.
