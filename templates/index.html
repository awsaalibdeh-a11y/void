Drive al-Tubaishi

  1. Drive anywhere: W A S D or the arrows. The real streets of al-Tubaishi, Dammam, with traffic and people.
  2. Stunts: the loop and the ramp are right in front of you. Too slow at the top of the loop and you fall.
  3. Fly: press F (or Fly). Space lifts, W boosts, F again to land. Land soft and keep driving.
  4. Missions: land on a green pad for points. G opens the garage.
